# Score provenance

The bundled score is **Come, Thou Fount of Every Blessing**, tune NETTLETON, in E-flat major, with four-part harmony. Text is attributed by its source to Robert Robinson, circa 1758; the tune is attributed there to Asahel Nettleton, published 1813. Tune attribution varies across historical sources.

Source page:
https://library.timelesstruths.org/music/Come_Thou_Fount_of_Every_Blessing/

Original MusicXML URL:
https://library.timelesstruths.org/library/music/C/Come_Thou_Fount_of_Every_Blessing/Come_Thou_Fount_of_Every_Blessing.xml

The source page identifies the text and music as public domain. The included file `scores/come-thou-fount.musicxml` preserves the downloaded score; `hymn.js` is a preprocessed representation used by this prototype.

The source file was retrieved during development on October 31, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). No recorded performance or commercial sound library is bundled.

## All Creatures of Our God and King

`scores/all-creatures.musicxml` is **All Creatures of Our God and King**, tune LASST UNS ERFREUEN, in D major, with four-part harmony. Its source attributes the words to Francis of Assisi, circa 1225, translated by William H. Draper, published 1919, altered; the tune is anonymous, published 1623; the harmony is by Ralph Vaughan Williams, published 1906.

Source page:
https://library.timelesstruths.org/music/All_Creatures_of_Our_God_and_King/

The source page identifies the text and music as public domain. Vaughan Williams died in 1958, so his harmony may still be under copyright outside the United States. The file is stored as downloaded on October 31, 2026 and is not yet playable in the app.
